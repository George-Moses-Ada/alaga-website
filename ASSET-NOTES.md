## Fonts

Font files were downloaded from Google Fonts stylesheets and are bundled locally. `assets/fonts.css` identifies each family and file mapping. Refer to the upstream license for each family when redistributing or modifying fonts:

- DM Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
- Italiana: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/italiana
- Material Symbols: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

Logo lettering, shop mockups, buttons and page decorations are HTML/CSS. No paid template, separate logo image, illustration package or frontend framework is required.
